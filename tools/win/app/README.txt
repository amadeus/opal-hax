Opal for Windows
================

Install: double-click Opal.exe and click OK. Windows asks for permission once
(it installs to Program Files and adds the "Opal C1" camera for video apps).

Then: plug in the Opal C1, click the round yellow Opal icon in the system tray
(it may be under the ^ arrow), and choose "Opal C1" as the camera in Zoom,
Teams, Meet, Discord, the Camera app, etc.

Uninstall: right-click the tray icon > Uninstall Opal...

Needs Windows 11. Problems: send %APPDATA%\Opal\opal.log
